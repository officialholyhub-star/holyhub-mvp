# HolyHub Stage 1 — Agreed decisions

- Build the product separately from the existing HolyHub landing page.
- Planned product address: `app.holyhub.co.uk` (subdomain of the existing domain).
- Existing landing page remains untouched for now.
- Stack: Next.js + TypeScript + PostgreSQL/Supabase.
- Stripe remains the payment provider, but Stripe integration is not part of Stage 1.
- One HolyHub login can have more than one role. Every signup begins as a customer; approved accounts can later gain lister access without creating another login.
- Brand direction: baby blue as the main colour, pink accents, clean/minimal layout, no filler UI.
- Current pricing rule for later stages: first 10 product listings free; additional listings £0.20 each; 5% sales commission.
- Refund/reserve decisions are intentionally deferred until the relevant payment/refund stage.
